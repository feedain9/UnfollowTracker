Privacy, in plain language.

Your Instagram lists stay in your browser. Here is exactly what UnfollowTracker does, and the only anonymous statistics we receive.

Data used by the extension

The extension reads the signed-in account ID, the IDs and names in your follower and following lists, their usernames and profile-image URLs. It uses the Instagram session and anti-CSRF token already present on instagram.com to perform the requests you initiate. It never asks for your password and does not store a copy of authentication cookies or tokens.

Storage and retention

The latest complete scan is stored in local extension storage, separately per Instagram account, until replaced, deleted from the panel or removed when the extension is uninstalled. In-progress scan state is kept in browser session storage. Language, theme and your statistics choice are stored locally, along with a few usage counters: install date, number of completed scans and saved accounts, outcome of the last scan and last day of use.

Anonymous usage statistics

Since version 1.2.0, the extension installed from the Chrome Web Store sends anonymous usage statistics. They tell us how many people install, use and uninstall the extension, and which scans fail, so we can improve it. The measured events are install, update, opening the panel (at most once a day), a scan starting, completing or failing, export, a confirmed unfollow and deleting saved scans.

Each event only contains the extension version, browser, interface language, an error category and value ranges such as “300–999 followers”, “10–49 non-reciprocal accounts” or “1–6 days since install”. It never contains your Instagram account ID or username, your lists, account names or photos, your cookies or tokens, or the address of pages you visit. No identifier tied to your browser or installation is created.

These statistics go to our own Plausible Analytics instance, a cookieless audience measurement tool hosted by Hetzner Online in Germany. On receipt, your IP address is only used to derive your country and compute an anonymous identifier that changes every day; Plausible does not keep it. We only look at aggregated results.

You can turn these statistics off at any time by unchecking “Share anonymous usage statistics” at the bottom of the panel. A version loaded outside the Chrome Web Store sends no statistics.

Install and uninstall pages

After installation, the extension opens a welcome page on this website. After an uninstall, your browser opens a page offering a short, optional survey. Its address includes the version, browser, install date, range of completed scans and outcome of the last scan; the page converts the date into a range before sending this information to our statistics, then removes it from the address bar. Your survey answer is only sent if you choose a reason. If you had turned statistics off, the address contains none of this information and nothing is sent automatically.

Recipients

Read requests and confirmed unfollows go directly to Instagram. Profile photos load from Instagram or Meta image servers. No lists are sent to an UnfollowTracker server: the publisher only receives the anonymous statistics described above. No advertising service is included in the extension.

Your choices

Scanning begins only when requested. Unfollowing requires individual confirmation. You can export non-reciprocal accounts as JSON, remove all saved lists using “Delete saved scans” and turn usage statistics off in the panel. Previously exported files remain on your device and must be deleted separately if you wish.

This website

This website sets no cookies and requires no login. It measures its audience with the same Plausible Analytics instance: pages viewed, referring site, campaign parameters, country, device type and browser, reading time, scrolling and clicks on install buttons. Page addresses are recorded without other parameters, and referring sites without their page path. No persistent identifier is used. The demonstration only uses fictional data. Fonts and images are hosted with the site. The hosting provider receives connection information, including your IP address and request logs; their handling depends on the hosting configuration and the provider’s policy linked below.

Legal basis

This audience measurement relies on our legitimate interest in understanding how the extension and website are used in order to improve them. It is limited to anonymous statistics, is never used for advertising or profiling, and is neither sold nor shared. You can object to the extension’s statistics in the panel, and to the website’s by blocking audience measurement scripts in your browser.
